RTL to GDSII Workshop Successfully Completed at ALIET Vijayawada

The 2-day intensive workshop “VLSI Design Fundamentals: RTL to GDSII using EDA Tools” was successfully concluded at ALIET (Autonomous) in collaboration with ElectroCratZ Technical Club. Guided by Mr. P V S D D Mallikarjuna, 16 student teams successfully designed and implemented 16 digital projects (ALU, Counters, Sequence Detectors) combining Cadence EDA tools and open-source toolchains.

Our Expertise

Pioneering the future of silicon technology through comprehensive research and robust solutions.

CMOS/VLSI Design

Cutting-edge methodologies in Very Large Scale Integration, ensuring high performance and low power consumption for modern electronic devices.

Design & Verification Exploration

We design basic chips from RTL to GDSII for other startups and MSMEs. End-to-end digital implementation flow, transforming Register-Transfer Level designs into physical GDSII layouts ready for fabrication.

Engineering Programs
