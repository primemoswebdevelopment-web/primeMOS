Research Internships

Semiconductor design, digital IC implementation, and academic publication preparation

Our intensive research internships provide a unique gateway into advanced electronic design automation (EDA) and silicon design methodologies. You will work on real-world digital VLSI flows from RTL description to physical verification, preparing research papers for peer-reviewed journals and top-tier IEEE/Springer conferences.

Apply Now

Program Structure

  • Duration: 3 to 5 Weeks
  • Mode: Fully Remote
  • Intake: Limited to 10 positions

Focus Areas

  • CMOS VLSI Design
  • Digital IC Design
  • EDA Tools & Integration
  • RTL to GDSII Flow
  • Internet of Things (IoT)

Eligibility

  • B.Tech (ECE, EEE, & related branches)
  • Students from 2nd to 7th Semester
  • Basic digital design knowledge
  • Age: 18 years or older (18+)

What You'll Gain

Hands-on experience with industry-standard EDA tools
Insight into circuit-level and system-level silicon design
Guidance in drafting and submitting research papers
A highly valuable research experience certificate

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this internship paid?

This is an unpaid research mentorship program. Participants receive a certificate and publication guidance upon successful completion.

Can CSE students apply?

The program is primarily designed for ECE, EEE, and related branches. CSE students with a strong interest in hardware and digital design may enquire separately.

Is prior VLSI knowledge required?

Basic digital design knowledge is preferred. Complete beginners may be considered on a case-by-case basis.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes, all participants who complete the program receive a PrimeMOS research experience certificate.

Are PrimeMOS student services and mentorship free?

We provide a mix of free and premium resources. Basic ECE career guidance, general webinars, and community support are 100% free. In-depth 1:1 project mentorship, specialized ECE laboratories, and custom CMOS simulation reviews are premium services micro-priced extremely affordably (starting from Rs. 10 up to Rs. 1000) to cover computational and support resources.

Important Notice: Admission is highly competitive and based strictly on academic profile and brief technical review.
