19 September 2026 VLSI Design Lab, GCET Hyderabad IETE ISF & ChipIN Centre 4.8 / 5.0 Feedback Rating

PrimeMOS Research & Solutions™ successfully conducted an intensive, highly rated hands-on workshop on "ECE to VLSI Chip Design Using Open-Source Tools" at Geethanjali College of Engineering and Technology (GCET), Keesara. The full-day session was led by Mr. P V S D D Mallikarjuna, Founder of PrimeMOS Research & Solutions™, serving as Resource Person.

The workshop was organized in close collaboration with the IETE ISF GCET, the Analog and Digital Electronics Group (Dept. of ECE, GCET), and the ChipIN Centre COE GCET under the national Chips to Start-up (C2S) Programme (supported by C-DAC Bangalore, an initiative of MeitY, Govt. of India).

Exceptional Impact: Seeing students go from "what is a MOSFET" to placing and routing their own designs in a single day — validated by a remarkable 4.8/5 feedback score!
Official MoU Proposal Under Process
Following the resounding success of the 19th September hands-on workshop, an official Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) proposal between PrimeMOS Research & Solutions™ and Geethanjali College of Engineering and Technology (GCET) is currently under active process to establish sustained VLSI design training, laboratory integration, faculty enablement, and joint semiconductor design exploration with GCET's ChipIN Centre (C2S Programme, MeitY).

28 June & 1 July 2026 Online · Interactive PrimeMOS & TapeoutX

PrimeMOS Research & Solutions, in collaboration with TapeoutX, successfully organized a comprehensive webinar on the Semiconductor Ecosystem & VLSI on 28th June, followed by a dedicated student Project Presentation & Q&A session on 1st July. The session brought together close to 90 ECE students and was led by Mr. P V S D D Mallikarjuna (Founder, PrimeMOS), Mr. Lingam Charan (Founder, TapeoutX), and Mr. Lakkimsetty Lakshmi Narasimha Swamy (Co-Founder, TapeoutX).

The sessions covered critical domains like RTL Design, Design Verification, Physical Design, DFT, STA, Fabrication, Embedded Systems, Communication, and Defence Electronics. On 1st July, eight students came back to voluntarily present their own project work, taking peer and mentor feedback.

24 - 28 August 2026 International · Virtual PrimeMOS × IEEE SB RRU

PrimeMOS Research & Solutions, in collaboration with IEEE Student Branch RRU, successfully wrapped up its 5-day international workshop on VLSI Design Using Open Source Tools. The event brought together students, faculty, and international participants (including attendees from the Philippines) for a deep dive into the semiconductor ecosystem.

Spanning five interactive days, the workshop covered comprehensive concepts of Open-Source EDA, RTL-to-GDSII workflow, Analog IC flow, CMOS fundamentals, and open-source VLSI design tools, empowering aspiring VLSI engineers with practical industry knowledge.

90+ Voices · Students, Faculty & International Participants
5 Days of Learning
Day 1: Introduction to VLSI & Semiconductor IC Design Covered VLSI fundamentals, IC design flow, EDA tools, memory & programmable logic.
Day 2: Deep Dive into VLSI Design & Workflows Covered MOSFET characteristics, CMOS logic gates, first steps into Verilog RTL.
Day 3: Exploring Open-Source EDA Tools Covered PTL & Transmission Gates, CS/CD amplifiers, building an ALU from scratch.
Day 4: RTL-to-GDSII Implementation (Part 1) Covered 45nm circuit design, complete RTL-to-GDSII flow, Linux-based EDA workflow.
Day 5: RTL-to-GDSII Conclusion & Feedback Covered physical design deep-dive, a full counter through synthesis & physical design, complete RTL → Synthesis → Physical Design → GDSII flow.
