Workshop on VLSI Design — VBIT Hyderabad

The Department of ECE at Vignan's Bharathi Institute of Technology (VBIT) successfully organized a comprehensive Workshop on VLSI Design for III Year ECE students. The session was led by PVSDD Mallikarjuna, Founder of PrimeMOS Research and Solutions, who shared valuable insights into VLSI design concepts, industry applications, and emerging trends in semiconductor technology.

Students gained practical exposure to VLSI fundamentals, CMOS circuit design principles, EDA tools, and RTL-to-GDSII design flow concepts. The event saw enthusiastic participation across both on-campus and online attendees.
