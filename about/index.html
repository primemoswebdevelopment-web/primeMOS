About PrimeMOS

PrimeMOS Research & Solutions creates opportunities for undergraduate and early postgraduate students to engage with semiconductor research, design exploration, simulation studies, and engineering-oriented technical activities.

About PrimeMOS

PrimeMOS™ Research & Solutions is an organization focused on semiconductor research, VLSI design, Electronic Design Automation (EDA), and engineering-oriented technical exploration. The organization aims to provide undergraduate and early postgraduate students with exposure to practical design workflows, simulation methodologies, and industry-relevant engineering concepts in core electronics and semiconductor domains.

PrimeMOS operates with a research-driven approach emphasizing CMOS design, RTL-to-GDS workflows, embedded systems, circuit analysis, and design-oriented problem solving aligned with evolving semiconductor technologies.
