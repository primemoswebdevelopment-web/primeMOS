Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

PrimeMOS™ Research & Solutions values user privacy and is committed to protecting personal information shared through this website.

1. Information Collected

The website may collect information voluntarily submitted by users, including:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Academic or professional details
  • Messages submitted through forms
  • Resume or project-related information

2. What We DO NOT Collect

PrimeMOS™ Research & Solutions does not collect, request, or store any of the following through this website or its forms:

  • Bank account numbers or banking credentials
  • Credit card or debit card details
  • UPI IDs or payment instrument credentials
  • Net banking login information
  • Any other financial or payment-related sensitive data

If you are ever asked for such information through any channel claiming to represent PrimeMOS™, please treat it as fraudulent and report it immediately.

3. Purpose of Information Collection

Collected information may be used for:

  • Responding to queries

  • Providing updates regarding services or activities

PrimeMOS™ does not intentionally sell, rent, or share personal information with unauthorized third parties.

5. Communication

Users who submit forms or contact requests may be contacted through email, WhatsApp, or other official communication channels for discussions related to services, workshops, webinars, mentorship, or technical opportunities.

6. Cookies and Analytics

The website may use basic cookies or analytics tools to understand visitor interaction and improve performance.

7. External Links

This website may contain references to external websites, journals, or platforms. PrimeMOS™ is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites.

8. Age Policy

PrimeMOS™ services, internships, mentorship programs, and student community channels are strictly intended for individuals who are 18 years of age or older (18+). We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personal information from anyone under the age of 18.

9. Consent

By accessing or using this website, users agree to the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy.

10. Policy Updates

PrimeMOS™ reserves the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time without prior notice.
