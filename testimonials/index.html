Official Institutional Letter
To: PrimeMOS Research & Solutions
Date: 6th October 2026
Subject: Letter of Appreciation – Two-Day Workshop on “Fundamentals of VLSI: RTL-to-GDSII Flow Using EDA Tools”

Dear PrimeMOS team,

“We sincerely appreciate PrimeMOS Research & Solutions for its valuable contribution as the Technical Resource Organization for the Two-Day Workshop on 'Fundamentals of VLSI: RTL-to-GDSII Flow Using EDA Tools', organized by the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering, in association with the ElectroCratz Club, at Andhra Loyola Institute of Engineering and Technology (Autonomous), Vijayawada, on 5th and 6th October 2026.”

“The workshop provided students with valuable exposure to the fundamentals of VLSI design and the RTL-to-GDSII flow using EDA tools, covering important concepts related to digital VLSI design, CMOS technology, RTL design, synthesis, physical design, and chip implementation.”

“The technical sessions, practical demonstrations, and hands-on guidance provided during the workshop helped the participants gain a better understanding of the practical aspects of VLSI design and the overall semiconductor design flow.”

“The efforts of PrimeMOS Research & Solutions, in collaboration with the Department of ECE and ElectroCratz Club, in promoting practical and concept-oriented learning in VLSI and EDA tools are sincerely appreciated.”

“We express our gratitude for your valuable contribution and technical support towards the successful conduct of the workshop. We wish PrimeMOS Research & Solutions continued success in its endeavors in the fields of VLSI, semiconductor design, research, and technical education.”

With Appreciation,
Head of the Department
Department of ECE, ALIET
